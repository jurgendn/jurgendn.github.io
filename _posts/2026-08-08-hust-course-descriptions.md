## Common sources for the remaining courses

The remaining courses do not currently have verified standalone public PDFs in HUST's indexed catalog. Use these official sources to locate course codes and, where available, read the corresponding catalogue description:

- [K62 Talent Program curriculum](https://fami.hust.edu.vn/ctdt-tai-nang-toan-tin-cho-cac-khoa-tu-k62/) — the authoritative course list, credits, and semester structure.
- [FaMI English course outlines](https://fami.hust.edu.vn/en/management-information-system-course-outlines/) — English objectives, prerequisites, and topic summaries for many shared courses.
- [FaMI Mathematics–Informatics summaries](https://fami.hust.edu.vn/tomtathocphancntoantin/) — Vietnamese course summaries.
- [FaMI engineering Mathematics–Informatics summaries](https://fami.hust.edu.vn/motahocphan_ks_toantin/) — Vietnamese descriptions for engineering and advanced courses.
- [FaMI MI programme specification](https://fami.hust.edu.vn/en/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/MI1-Programme-specification.pdf) — English programme-level course and learning-outcome context.

For an application, use the K62 curriculum for transcript facts and label descriptions from related programmes as supporting context rather than as the exact K62 syllabus.
